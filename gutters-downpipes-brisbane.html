Why Replace Your Gutters

Good Gutters Protect Your Entire Home

Gutters are the first line of defence against Queensland's intense rainfall events. When gutters rust, overflow, or pull away from fascia, water cascades down your walls, seeps into your sub-floor, and begins eroding your home's foundations. Gutter failure is one of the leading causes of preventable structural damage in South East Queensland.

Ascend Roofing Group installs premium Colorbond® seamless gutters and downpipes matched precisely to your roof colour and profile — providing decades of reliable water management.

Custom-Matched Colorbond®

Gutters, downpipes, and fascia are all colour-matched to your existing Colorbond® roof in all 22 standard colours.

Correct Downpipe Sizing

Undersized downpipes are a major cause of overflow in Queensland storms. We calculate the correct number and size for your roof area and rainfall intensity.

Fascia Inspection Included

We check your fascia board condition before installation — rotted fascia must be replaced to ensure gutters are properly secured.

What We Install

Gutter Profiles & Options

We supply and install all standard gutter profiles in premium Colorbond® steel — matched to your home's style, roof pitch, and drainage needs.

Quad Gutters

The most common gutter profile in Queensland. A classic quarter-round profile available in all 22 Colorbond® colours. Suited to most residential roof pitches.

Half Round Gutters

A deeper, semi-circular profile providing superior flow capacity for steep roofs or large catchment areas — particularly suited to heritage Queensland homes.

Squareline Gutters

A contemporary square-section gutter for modern homes. Clean architectural lines complement modern Colorbond® and rendered facades perfectly.

Downpipes

Correctly-sized round or square Colorbond® downpipes installed at correct intervals. We calculate the minimum number of outlets required per AS 3500.3 plumbing standards.

Fascia Replacement

Rotted or termite-damaged fascia boards are replaced before gutter installation to ensure secure fixing. We use treated pine or aluminium fascia as appropriate.

Colorbond® Ultra (Coastal)

Gold Coast homes within 1km of the ocean require Colorbond® Ultra gutters and downpipes — engineered with enhanced salt-spray resistance. We specify and supply correctly.
