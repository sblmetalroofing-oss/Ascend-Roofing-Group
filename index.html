Common FAQ's

Got a Question About How We Operate?

How much does a Colorbond roof replacement cost in Brisbane?

In 2026, most standard Brisbane homes fall between $15,000 and $26,000 for a Colorbond roof replacement, with the full range running from $12,000 to $45,000+ depending on roof size, pitch, and whether structural tie-downs are required.

Do you offer free roofing quotes in Queensland?

Yes! We provide 100% free, no-obligation roof inspections and comprehensive quotes across Brisbane, the Gold Coast, Logan, and Ipswich. Request a quote and we'll book a time that suits you.

Can you replace a tile roof with a metal Colorbond roof?

Absolutely. This is our primary specialty. We safely remove your old heavy concrete or terracotta tiles, install new cyclonic tie-downs and structural battens, and lay down lightweight premium Colorbond steel, drastically reducing the structural weight on your home's frame.
