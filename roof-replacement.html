Is It Time?

Signs Your Brisbane Home Needs a Roof Replacement

Queensland's storms, hail, and relentless UV take a heavy toll. Patching a failing roof only delays the inevitable and often costs more in the long run. If your roof is showing these signs, an upgrade to Colorbond® steel is the smartest investment:

Constant Leaks

Water stains on ceilings or active dripping during heavy Queensland downpours.

Cracked or Fretting Tiles

Concrete/terracotta tiles that crumble, shift, or break under hail and foot traffic.

Asbestos Presence

Dangerous Super Six fibro roofing that needs immediate, licensed removal and disposal.

Sagging or Bowing Roofline

Structural issues caused by the severe weight of old, waterlogged tiles.

Colorbond® Metal Roof Replacement Brisbane

Colorbond® steel is Australia's number-one roofing material for good reason — it's lightweight, cyclone-rated, and carries up to a 36-year warranty when professionally installed. We replace old corrugated iron and aged Colorbond® with new BlueScope® Colorbond® in all 22 standard colours.

Choose your colour with our interactive colour guide or we'll bring physical samples to your property. See our full 2026 roof replacement cost guide for pricing.

🔄

Tile to Metal Conversions Brisbane & Gold Coast

Tile-to-metal roof replacement is our most popular service across Brisbane, Logan, and the Gold Coast. Heavy concrete and terracotta tiles cause structural overloading, are prone to cracking, and require constant maintenance in Queensland's intense UV and storm environment.

We safely remove all tiles, upgrade your structural tie-downs to current Queensland cyclone standards, lay new Colorbond® sheeting with Anticon® insulation, and install matching gutters and downpipes. An all-in-one solution — see our insulation page and gutter installation page for details on those inclusions.

⚠️

Asbestos Roof Removal & Safe Replacement

Homes built before 1990 in Queensland often have Super Six fibro (asbestos cement) roofing. Disturbing asbestos without proper precautions is a serious health hazard and illegal in Queensland. We coordinate fully licensed Class-B asbestos removalists to safely remove and EPA-dispose of all asbestos material before your new Colorbond® roof is installed.

We manage the entire process — asbestos report, licensed removal, EPA disposal documentation, and new roof installation — so you don't have to coordinate multiple contractors. Request a quote and we'll advise on the full process.

🔩

Galvanised, Zincalume® & Corrugated Iron Replacement

Old galvanised or Zincalume® corrugated iron roofing eventually suffers from pinhole rusting, failed screws, and silicone-patched leak points that are beyond economic repair. We replace these roofs with modern Colorbond® steel on the same batten line — a straightforward like-for-like upgrade.

For properties near the coast, we specify Colorbond® Ultra with enhanced salt-spray resistance to meet the demanding environment of the Gold Coast beachside and Moreton Bay suburbs. See our full service areas.
