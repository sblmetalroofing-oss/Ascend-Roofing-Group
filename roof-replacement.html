Is It Time for a Full Roof Replacement in Brisbane?

Queensland's severe storms, hail, and relentless sun take a heavy toll. Patching a failing roof only delays the inevitable and often costs more in the long run. If your roof is showing these signs, an upgrade to Colorbond® steel is the smartest investment:

  • Constant Leaks: Water stains on ceilings or active dripping during heavy QLD downpours.
  • Cracked or Fretting Tiles: Concrete/terracotta tiles that crumble under foot traffic or hail.
  • Severe Rust: Extensive corrosion on existing metal roofing, especially near the Gold Coast shore.
  • Sagging Roofline: Structural issues caused by the severe weight of old, waterlogged tiles.
  • Asbestos Presence: Highly dangerous Super Six roofing that needs immediate, safe removal.

Why Tile to Metal Roof Replacement is Brisbane's #1 Choice

Replacing an old tile roof with a modern Colorbond metal roof completely transforms the look and safety of your home.

  • Unmatched Durability: Tested against harsh Australian conditions. Won't crack under hail like terracotta or cement tiles.
  • Thermal Efficiency: Coupled with Anticon insulation, it reflects solar heat, drastically reducing your summer cooling bills.
  • Lightweight: Up to 90% lighter than a tiled roof, alleviating massive structural strain on your home's framing.
  • Watertight Security: Forget about slipped tiles or crumbling pointing. Metal sheets offer superior protection against torrential rain.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a roof replacement take?

Most residential roof replacements in Queensland take between 2 to 4 days, depending on the weather and roof complexity. We always ensure your home is watertight at the end of every work day.

Can you replace an old tile roof with Colorbond?

Absolutely. Tile to metal roof replacement is our specialty. We safely remove the tiles, install new heavy-duty tie-downs and battens to meet QLD cyclone standards, and lay down lightweight Colorbond steel.

Do you handle asbestos roof removal?

Yes, we work alongside fully licensed class-B asbestos removalists to safely remove and dispose of old Super Six asbestos roofing before installing your brand new roof.

Do I need to move out during the roof replacement?

No, you can stay in your home. There will be some noise during working hours as we remove materials and secure the new battening and steel, but the interior of your home remains completely untouched and safe.
