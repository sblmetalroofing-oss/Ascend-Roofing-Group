Light & Ventilation

A Cooler, Brighter Home Starts at the Roof

A metal or tile roof in direct Queensland sun acts like an oven lid — trapping heat in your roof cavity and radiating it into your ceiling and living areas. The solution isn't just more air conditioning — it's properly removing that heat at the source with Whirlybird roof ventilators, then filling those spaces with natural daylight through a well-placed skylight.

Ascend Roofing Group installs both systems with the same precision and care as a full roof installation — correctly flashed, weathertight, and backed by our workmanship warranty.

Weathertight Flashing — Guaranteed

Every skylight and Whirlybird is fitted with custom-fabricated lead or aluminium flashings to ensure zero water penetration.

Works on All Roof Types

Colorbond steel, corrugated iron, concrete tiles, terracotta tiles — we fabricate the right flashing for every roof profile.

Best Combined With Insulation

Whirlybirds and Anticon® insulation work together — insulation slows heat entry while Whirlybirds expel heat that builds up in the cavity.

What We Install

Skylight & Ventilation Products & Services

We supply and install quality skylights and Whirlybird ventilators to suit every roof type and budget across Brisbane and the Gold Coast.

Fixed Skylights

Flat or domed fixed skylights bring permanent natural daylight into hallways, bathrooms, and living areas that can't be easily window-lit. Available in polycarbonate and glass glazing options.

Operable Skylights

Manually or electrically operated skylights that open for ventilation as well as light. A popular choice for kitchens and living areas — adds light, allows hot air to escape, and brings in fresh breeze.

Solatube® Tubular Skylights

Sun tunnels that pipe daylight through a reflective tube into rooms with no direct roof access — ideal for interior bathrooms, hallways, and rooms under an upper storey. Minimal structural impact.

Whirlybird Ventilators (300mm)

Wind-powered rotary ventilators that continuously expel hot air from your roof cavity without electricity. One 300mm unit ventilates approximately 50–60m² of roof space. Colorbond® colour-matched options available.

Whirlybird Ventilators (400mm)

Larger diameter ventilators for homes with a bigger roof cavity or higher heat loads. Ideal for large single-storey homes, workshops, sheds, and commercial properties across South East Queensland.

Skylight Replacement

Old, cracked, or UV-yellowed skylight domes are replaced with modern polycarbonate or glass units — dramatically improving light output and eliminating leaks from failed flashing on aged units.
