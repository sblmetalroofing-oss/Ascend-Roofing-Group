Common FAQ's

Got a Question About How We Operate?

How much does a Colorbond roof replacement cost in Brisbane?

Cost depends on roof size, pitch, access and whether the existing roof is tile or metal. We inspect the roof first, then give you a written quote with no obligation to proceed.

Do you offer free roofing quotes in Queensland?

Yes. Free roof inspection and a written quote, with no obligation and no surprises.

Can you replace a tile roof with a metal Colorbond roof?

Absolutely — this is our specialty. We safely remove your old concrete or terracotta tiles, install new cyclonic tie-downs and battens, and lay down lightweight premium Colorbond steel.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes — QBCC licensed (Lic. 15600031) and fully insured on every job.
