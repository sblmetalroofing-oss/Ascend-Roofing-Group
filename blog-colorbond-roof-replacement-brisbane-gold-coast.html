Is It Time?

Signs Your Brisbane Roof Needs Replacing in 2026

Queensland's storms, hail, and relentless UV take a punishing toll on roofs. Patching a failing roof is often a false economy — water ingress can rot timber framing, destroy ceilings, and compromise electrical wiring. Watch for these warning signs:

Constant Leaks

New wet patches on your ceiling after every storm mean the roof system has failed — not just a cracked tile.

Fretting or Cracked Tiles

Concrete and terracotta tiles become brittle over decades. If they crumble underfoot, they won't survive the next hailstorm.

Sagging Roofline

Waterlogged tiles add enormous weight. A bowing ridge line is a structural red flag requiring immediate action.

Asbestos (Super Six) Sheeting

Homes built before 1990 with corrugated cement sheeting very likely contain asbestos. This needs safe, licensed removal — we handle everything.

Why Metal Wins

Why Tile-to-Metal is Queensland's #1 Choice

90% of our replacement clients choose Colorbond® steel over tile. Here's why the advantages in Queensland's climate are undeniable.

01

Tested for Extremes

Manufactured in Australia, Colorbond® is rigorously tested against hail, blistering heat, and torrential rain. It won't shatter under impact like terracotta or cement tiles.

02

Up to 90% Lighter Than Tile

Metal roofing removes enormous structural strain from your home's timber framing — actually making the entire structure more resilient over time.

03

Thermal Efficiency

Paired with premium Anticon blanket insulation, lighter Colorbond® shades reflect solar radiation — slashing summer air conditioning costs dramatically.

04

Watertight Security

No more slipped tiles or crumbling pointing. Interlocking metal sheets provide far superior protection against Queensland's tropical downpours.

05

22 Designer Colours

From classic Surfmist® to bold Monument®, a new Colorbond® roof instantly modernises your home's kerb appeal and adds real property value.

06

Up to 30-Year Warranty

BlueScope Steel manufactures Colorbond® to last. Combined with Ascend's QBCC-licensed workmanship warranty, your investment is fully protected.
