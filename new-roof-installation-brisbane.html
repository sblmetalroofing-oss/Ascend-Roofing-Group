What We Install

Colorbond® Profiles & Systems

We install every major Colorbond® profile — your builder or architect specifies it, we deliver it perfectly.

Custom Orb® (Corrugated)

Australia's most iconic profile. Classic corrugated steel in all 22 Colorbond® colours. Perfect for traditional Queensland homes and Queenslander restorations.

Trimdek® & Hi-Ten®

Trapezoidal rib profiles offering superior strength for lower-pitched roofs. Popular for modern homes and extensions across Brisbane and the Gold Coast.

Klip-Lok® 700 (Concealed Fix)

Standing-seam concealed fix system for a sleek, fastener-free appearance. The premium choice for contemporary architect-designed homes.

Colorbond® Ultra (Coastal)

Enhanced corrosion resistance for homes within 1km of the ocean or estuaries. Mandatory for many Gold Coast properties — we specify and install correctly.

Anticon® Insulation Blanket

Every new install includes Anticon® thermal blanket as standard. Dramatically reduces heat transfer — a must in Queensland's subtropical climate.

Gutters, Fascia & Flashings

Complete install means watertight edges. We fabricate and fit custom-matched Colorbond® gutters, fascia, valleys, and flashings on every job.
