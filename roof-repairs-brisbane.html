Repair Types

Roof Repairs We Specialise In

From a single cracked tile to full storm-damage rectification — we handle every type of roof repair across Brisbane and the Gold Coast.

Leak Detection & Repair

Wet ceilings, water stains, or damp walls after rain? We use drone and close-up inspection to locate the exact entry point and seal it permanently — not temporarily.

Cracked & Broken Tile Replacement

Cracked, slipped, or missing concrete and terracotta tiles are replaced with matching materials. We carry a wide range of tile profiles and colours for seamless repairs.

Ridge Cap Repointing & Resealing

Crumbling ridge cap mortar is one of Queensland's most common leak causes. We remove the old pointing, replace damaged caps, and reseal with flexible weather-resistant compound.

Storm Damage Repairs

Hail impacts, fallen tree limbs, or wind-lifted sheets — we inspect the full extent of damage, document it for your insurer if required, and restore your roof quickly.

Gutter & Downpipe Repairs

Blocked, rusted, or overflowing gutters direct water back under your fascia. We clear, reline, or replace gutters and downpipes to restore proper drainage. See our gutter service page.

Colorbond® Sheet Repairs

Rusted screws, wind-lifted panels, cracked flashings, or damaged valleys on your metal roof — all repaired using matching Colorbond® material with cyclone-rated fixings.
