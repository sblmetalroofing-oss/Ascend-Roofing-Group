Repair or Replace? 7 Signs Your Brisbane Roof Is Past the Point of Patching

Queensland's harsh UV, subtropical storms, and cyclone-season winds put roofs under relentless stress. Over time, even a solid roof reaches a point where continued repairs cost more than a full replacement and keep you in a cycle of recurring leaks. After 1,200+ roof jobs across Brisbane, Gold Coast, Logan, and Ipswich, our team knows exactly when repair stops making sense.

The 7 Warning Signs

Sign 1: You've Had More Than Two Leaks or Repairs in Five Years

One repair is normal. Two in quick succession might be bad luck. But if you're calling a roofer every 12–18 months, the roof structure itself has degraded past what individual patches can solve. Every dollar spent repairing an end-of-life roof is money that could go toward a new Colorbond® with a 36-year warranty. See our roof replacement guide.

Sign 2: Your Roof Is Over 25 Years Old

Expected lifespans in Queensland's climate: concrete tiles 20–30 years, terracotta 30–50 (but brittle), corrugated iron/galvanised 15–25 years. If your roof is at or past these thresholds, a proactive replacement beats reactive repairs.

Sign 3: Visible Rust, Widespread Cracking, or Missing Sections

Localised rust on one sheet or a single broken tile can be repaired. But if you see widespread rust patches, multiple slipped or cracked tiles, or sections of material missing, the damage has progressed beyond targeted repair. Storm season will only make it worse.

Sign 4: Water Stains on Interior Ceilings or Walls

Yellow-brown ceiling stains mean water is getting through. Beyond the roof itself, this risks timber rot, mould in insulation, and gyprock damage. Get a professional assessment — if the structural cause is widespread, replacement is the safer long-term decision. Our team can assess and advise.

Sign 5: Your Roof Contains Asbestos (Super Six Fibro)

Pre-1986 Brisbane and Ipswich homes commonly have Super Six asbestos cement sheeting. As it ages, it becomes friable. Queensland law requires a licensed Class-B removalist — you cannot repair, drill, cut, or pressure-wash it yourself. If it's deteriorating, replacement is legally required. We coordinate the full process. Read our asbestos roof removal guide.

Sign 6: Your Cooling Bills Have Been Rising Without Explanation

An aged, uninsulated roof is one of Queensland's biggest energy wasters. If summer electricity bills are climbing without explanation, your roof may be absorbing and radiating significantly more heat than it should. A new Colorbond® with Anticon® insulation can deliver measurable reductions in cooling costs.

Sign 7: Your Roof Doesn't Meet Current Queensland Wind Standards

Queensland's wind-rating codes have been significantly upgraded since major cyclone events. Pre-2000 roofs may have inadequate tie-downs and batten systems that won't hold in a severe storm. If tiles have lifted or you've lost ridge capping in high winds, a full re-roof to current cyclone-rated standards is the safest solution.

FAQ — Brisbane Roof Replacement vs. Repairs

How do I know if my Brisbane roof needs replacing or just repairs?

If repairs are needed more than once per year, the roof is over 25 years old, there's widespread damage, or you have asbestos, full replacement is almost always more cost-effective. A free on-site assessment gives you a clear, honest picture.

How much does roof replacement cost vs. repairs in Brisbane?

Repairs typically cost $500–$3,000. A full Colorbond® replacement costs $12,000–$45,000+. If you're spending over $3,000 on repairs on a roof over 20 years old, replacement almost always wins in long-term value. Read our 2026 cost guide.

What is the lifespan of a tile roof in Brisbane?

Concrete tiles last 20–30 years in Brisbane. Colorbond® carries a 36-year manufacturer warranty and routinely lasts 40–50+ years with minimal maintenance — a significantly better long-term investment.
