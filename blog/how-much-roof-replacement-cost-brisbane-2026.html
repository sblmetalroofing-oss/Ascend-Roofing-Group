How Much Does a Roof Replacement Cost in Brisbane in 2026?

One of the most common questions we get from Brisbane and Gold Coast homeowners is: "What is a roof replacement going to cost me?" It's a fair question — and an important one. Unfortunately, many roofing websites give vague ranges or avoid the question entirely.

At Ascend Roofing Group, transparency is a core part of how we work. This guide gives you realistic, up-to-date cost ranges based on what we actually quote and complete across South East Queensland in 2026 — so you can budget properly before you pick up the phone.

What Affects Roof Replacement Cost in Brisbane?

The final quote you receive will depend on a combination of factors. Here's what drives cost up or down:

1. Roof Size (m²)

The single biggest factor. Roofing is priced per square metre for labour and materials. A 120m² roof costs significantly less than a 250m² roof. Most Brisbane suburban homes fall between 140–220m².

2. Roof Type — Tile, Metal, or Asbestos

Metal-to-metal replacements are the most straightforward. We remove old sheets and re-fix new Colorbond® on the existing batten frame.

Tile-to-metal conversions involve removing heavy concrete or terracotta tiles (skip bin required), then installing new cyclone-rated tie-downs and battens before laying Colorbond® sheeting. The structural upgrade adds labour time and cost but is a complete, long-term solution. See our detailed tile-to-metal conversion guide.

Asbestos (Super Six fibro) roofs require licensed Class-B removalists and EPA disposal. This adds a separate contractor cost that typically ranges from $4,000–$8,000 depending on quantity and access.

3. Roof Pitch and Complexity

Steeper pitches require more safety equipment, slower installation, and more material waste from cutting. A standard low-pitch gable roof costs less than a high-pitch hip roof or one with multiple valley lines and dormers.

4. Accessibility and Height

Single-storey homes with clear access cost less to scaffold than double-storey homes near fences or gardens. Scaffolding is always included in our quotes — we never use unsecured ladders on a complete re-roof.

5. Insulation

Anticon® blanket insulation is the most cost-effective when installed during a re-roof — the blanket is laid before sheets go on, adding $2,000–$5,000. The payback in energy savings is typically 3–5 years. See our roof insulation page.

6. Gutters and Downpipes

Most complete roof replacements include new matching Colorbond® gutters and downpipes. If your existing gutters are in good condition, we may retain them — but for a tile-to-metal conversion, new gutters are always included. See our gutter replacement page.

Colorbond® Colours and Cost in Brisbane 2026

All 22 standard Colorbond® colours carry the same price point for standard residential roofing. Colorbond® Ultra (required within 1km of saltwater for coastal Gold Coast properties) is priced slightly higher. Colorbond® with Thermatech® technology (all lighter colours) may reduce your long-term cooling costs significantly.

We bring colour swatches to your property so you can match against your brickwork and fascia in natural light. Browse available Colorbond® colours online or call us to arrange a sample visit.

Roof Replacement Cost FAQ — Brisbane & Gold Coast 2026

How much does a roof replacement cost per m² in Brisbane?

As a rough guide, expect $80–$150 per m² fully installed for a standard Colorbond® metal roof replacement in Brisbane. Tile-to-metal conversions typically run $100–$180 per m² due to the additional structural work. These figures include all labour, materials, scaffolding, and removal. Always get a fixed-price quote before committing.

Is roof replacement cheaper during off-peak seasons in Queensland?

Demand is slightly lower during autumn and winter in South East Queensland (March–August), when storm season subsides. Booking during this period sometimes allows for slightly shorter lead times. Pricing is generally consistent year-round for reputable contractors — unusually cheap quotes often indicate shortcuts on materials or licensing.

Can I get a roof replacement on finance?

Many homeowners in Brisbane and the Gold Coast use personal or home equity loans for roof replacements. Some roofing contractors offer payment plan arrangements — contact us to discuss your situation. A full replacement typically qualifies for home improvement financing through your bank.

How long does a roof replacement take in Brisbane?

Most standard residential replacements take 2–4 business days. Larger or more complex roofs may take up to a week. The roof is always made weathertight at the end of each day — you'll never be left without protection overnight.

Does roof replacement add value to my Brisbane home?

Yes — significantly. A new Colorbond® roof is one of the highest-return improvements for resale value in Queensland. Real estate agents consistently cite roofing condition as a top factor in buyer confidence and property valuation. A new roof also allows you to pass a building inspection with flying colours.
